Lost in
Translation

A book about words with no English equivalent, typeset so the reader keeps losing something on the way across the gutter.

RoleDesign & typesetting
Year2021
MediumPrint — InDesign, Illustrator
OutputPerfect-bound book
EditorialTypography

Untranslatable words — mono no aware, saudade, hiraeth — sit inside a familiar serif body text that is repeatedly interrupted. A word breaks across the spread, a definition runs into the margin and stops, a page withholds the term it spent two paragraphs setting up. The typography is the argument, not the decoration: the reader is made to feel the loss the book is describing.

In hand

Offset printed and perfect bound. The interruptions only land at full size, in sequence, in the hand.
