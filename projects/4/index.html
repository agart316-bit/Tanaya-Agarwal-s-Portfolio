ARTSYDESIGN.CO

Year : 2020 Skills : Illustration, Adobe, Production

Medium : A5 book designed using Adobe Illustrator & Photoshop

During my time at ArtsyDesign.co, I worked on a teenage stationery line built around a unicorn theme. The target audience was teenage girls, so everything about it needed to feel bold, playful, and full of colour. I was involved across the whole process: research, illustration, the product photoshoot, and social media.

The line was vibrant by design. Hot pinks, purples, gold accents, bold stripes and patterns. The kind of stationery that actually makes you want to use it. I illustrated the artwork for the collection and then shot and styled the promotional imagery to bring it all together for the launch.
