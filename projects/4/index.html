Brand Identity

A cohesive stationery brand built around editorial restraint — every weight, texture, and ink choice is deliberate. ArtsyDesign.co translates tactile print traditions into a contemporary identity system.

Designed at A5, the collection spans notebooks, card sets, and gift packaging. Illustrator handles the vector precision; Photoshop layers in the papery warmth.

Process

Tactile Rhythm &
Typographic Restraint

Every element in the ArtsyDesign.co system was chosen for what it withholds as much as what it shows. Tight leading. Generous margin. The line as both separator and statement.

The ruled-line motif — borrowed from the notebooks themselves — carries through into every touchpoint, unifying print and digital.
