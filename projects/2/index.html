Type & Motion

Now Or
Never

A mantra taken literally: a sticker small enough to live in the moment, and a neon sign that flickers like it has been on too long.

RoleDesign & animation
Year2020
MediumPrint + motion — Illustrator, Photoshop
OutputSticker and looping neon sign
TypographyMotion

I chose Now or Never because it felt less like a quote to live by and more like something glowing above a bar on a Friday night — happy hour, going out, putting yourself out there. For print I went small on purpose: a sticker you put on a laptop or a bar hands out, living in the moment without making a statement of it.

Flickering on purpose

The motion piece is a glitching hot-pink neon sign, referencing the neon aesthetic that was everywhere around 2016. It stutters and drops out like a tube that has been lit too long, which is the whole argument of the phrase — the thing is about to go out, so decide now.

In motion

The loop as it plays — hot pink on black, flickering off beat.
