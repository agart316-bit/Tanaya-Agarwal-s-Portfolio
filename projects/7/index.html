A secondary-school physics textbook rebuilt on a modular grid, where the diagrams lead and the type gets out of the way.

RoleDesign & typesetting
Year2022
MediumPrint — InDesign, Illustrator
OutputFull textbook redesign
EditorialSystemsTypography

Rigorous content presented through a clear modular grid. Scientific diagrams are treated as primary visual elements rather than illustrations dropped beside the text, and a precise typographic hierarchy holds the reading order. Each chapter opener uses large-scale numerals and full-bleed field photography to ground abstract concepts in something tangible.

One grid, every page type

The same grid carries a chapter opener, a dense diagram spread, running body text, worked problems and the index. The modules change and the measure does not, which is what lets a student move between a vector diagram and a page of prose without re-learning where to look.
